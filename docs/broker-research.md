# What Pakistani brokers post: research notes (October 2026)

Sources: broker X accounts (Topline, JS Global), broker websites (AHL), and the KSE Alert research
aggregator (ksealert.com/Research), which republishes each broker's daily notes with summary and PDF.

## Daily rhythm across brokers

- 07:30 to 09:15: morning notes and technical outlooks (support/resistance, picks).
- 16:30 to 19:00: market reviews (close, range, drivers, volume, movers).
- Within hours of a data release: economy alerts (trade, CPI, reserves, debt, ratings).
- Friday evening / weekend: weekly reviews and outlooks.
- Before CPI and MPC: previews and surveys.

## How we use this (our own, branded, accurate)

1. Same facts, from the original source, in our template: market numbers from PSX/SCS, CPI and SPI from
   PBS, rates, reserves and auctions from SBP. These are public facts and fully ours to publish.
2. Broker views are always credited by name ("AKD: support at 166,700"), never presented as ours.
   A daily "Broker Views" card collects the morning and evening notes from KSE Alert with the broker name.
3. Broker forecasts (CPI, policy rate) feed Street View, which scores them against actual results.
4. Not copied: broker posters, logos, layouts or full report text.
